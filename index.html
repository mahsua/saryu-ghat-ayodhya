Ancient Heritage & Mythology

Where Mother Saryu Blesses the Holy Land of Awadh

Flowing gently beside Ayodhya in Uttar Pradesh, the Saryu River (also written Sarayu) is venerated as one of the seven holiest waters in Hindu thought. She is not merely a geographic channel, but an eternal witness to the epochs of the Treta Yuga.

Scriptural tradition recounts that Lord Rama performed His daily ablutions along these very steps. It was here, upon the conclusion of His earthly manifestation, that Lord Rama entered the sacred current for His Mahaprayana (Jal Samadhi) to return to Vaikuntha.

Spiritual Protocol

Pilgrim Routine at Saryu

Parampara
  1. 1
    Pratah Snan (Dawn Dip):

    Bathing between 5:00 AM and 7:00 AM using designated safety chains at Naya Ghat.

  2. 2
    Deep Daan (Lamp Offering):

    Offering flower-filled leaf bowls (donas) with cotton wicks into the river flow.

  3. 3
    Hanuman Garhi & Ram Janmabhoomi Darshan:

    Proceeding directly from the ghat to seek permission from Lord Hanuman and darshan of Ram Lalla.

  4. 4
    Sandhya Maha Aarti & Laser Show:

    Gathering on the stone steps of Ram Ki Paidi as the sun sinks into the western horizon.

Sanctity & Safety First

Visitor Etiquette & Safety Rules

✓
Always bathe within safety chains:

Water currents accelerate beyond the metal barrier ropes. Never attempt to cross into deep central navigation channels.

✓
Wear life jackets aboard boats:

All official boatmen are mandated by the district administration to issue life vests before pushing off.

✓
Remove footwear before steps:

Please deposit shoes at free municipal footwear counters before walking down to bathing steps or prayer podiums.

✕
Zero tolerance for plastic pollution:

Help keep Mother Saryu clean. Do not discard plastic bags, wrappers, soaps, or synthetic materials into the waters.

Transit & Location

How to Reach Saryu Ghat

Ayodhya is seamlessly connected across India by high-speed rail, air, and 4-lane national expressways.

By Air (Airports)

Maharishi Valmiki International Airport (AYJ): Located ~10 km away. Connected via app-cabs, pre-paid taxis, and electric autos (approx 20 mins).

Alternative: Chaudhary Charan Singh International Airport, Lucknow (LKO), ~145 km via NH 27.

Taxi Fare: ~₹300–₹500 from AYJ

By Train (Railways)

Ayodhya Dham Junction (AY): Only ~2.5 km from Naya Ghat. Accessible via battery e-rickshaw in 10 minutes.

Ayodhya Cantt Junction (AYC): ~10 km away; handles major long-distance Superfast & Vande Bharat express trains.

E-Rickshaw: ₹20–₹30 shared

By Road & City Transit

Directly connected via NH 27 (Lucknow-Gorakhpur highway). Ayodhya Central Bus Stand is roughly 3.5 km away with inter-state UPSRTC luxury & regular bus services.

Within town, eco-friendly e-rickshaws run continuously along the paved Ram Path directly to Naya Ghat.

Direct Highway Link via NH 27
